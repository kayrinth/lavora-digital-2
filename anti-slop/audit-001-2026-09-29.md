# antislop audit 001 — copy & text
Date: 2026-09-29
Scope: `antislop` core + `antislop-copywriting`, applied to visible copy in
`src/components/sections.tsx`, `src/components/newsletter.tsx`, `src/app/layout.tsx`.
Mode: AFTER (audit). Nothing is changed until you pick numbers.

## HIGH — Hard Gate (absolute)

1. **Fabricated statistics** `sections.tsx:153-154` — R-17, R-36, C-5
   "2.4B+ Impressions served" and "900+ Campaigns launched" have no source.
   Fix: remove the stat block, or replace with real figures you supply.

2. **Fabricated testimonial** `sections.tsx:166-174` — R-18, R-36, R-38, C-5
   "Paul Yayuk Reyhan, CMO of Northwind" plus the claim "Cost per acquisition dropped 41%".
   Invented person, invented company, invented number.
   Fix: drop the quote block, or swap in a real client quote with permission.

3. **Fake success state on the newsletter** `newsletter.tsx:10-13, 33` — R-26, R-38
   Submit sets `done` and the button says "Subscribed", but nothing is sent anywhere.
   The copy claims an action that did not happen.
   Fix: wire it to a real list, or change the button copy and label the form "Coming soon".

4. **Em dashes in copy** `sections.tsx:119`, `layout.tsx:8`, `layout.tsx:10` — R-02
   Three em dash characters in text the agent wrote.
   Fix: split into two sentences, or use a comma or colon.

5. **Ghost links in nav and footer** `sections.tsx:21-33, 626-633, 682` — R-24, R-26, R-38
   "Work" and the four service dropdown items all point at `#services`; "About" points at `#process`;
   every footer item including About, Case Studies, Careers, Terms and Privacy points at `#contact`.
   The labels promise pages that do not exist.
   Fix: cut the labels with no destination, or build the sections they name.

6. **Unverified contact address** `sections.tsx:616` — R-36, R-38
   `hello@lavoradigital.com` was written by me in an earlier turn and never confirmed with you.
   Fix: give me the real address, or I mark it `[REAL DATA]`.

## MEDIUM — Purpose-Gate (technique fine, reason must be written)

7. **Uppercase footer headings with wide tracking** `sections.tsx:641, 657` — R-06
   `tracking-[0.12em] uppercase` on SERVICES / COMPANY / CONNECT / STAY UPDATED is the
   named AI typography tell. Keep it only if you write down the brand reason.

8. **Tailing negation** `sections.tsx:186` — copywriting: Negative Parallelism
   "Audiences built from first-party data, never guesswork". The "never X" tail is a model rhythm.
   Fix: "Audiences built from your own first-party data."

9. **Aphorism formula** `sections.tsx:569-570` — copywriting: Aphorism Formulas
   "Great targeting only pays off when the creative earns the click and the landing page earns the sale."
   Sounds profound, states a generality.
   Fix: "Targeting gets the impression. Creative and the landing page get the sale."

## LOW — Quality Locks

10. **Empty-vocabulary headline verb** `sections.tsx:108` — R-16
    "Amplify your brand to every audience" sits in the same family as unlock / elevate / empower,
    and says nothing about what you do.
    Fix: a headline naming the actual outcome, e.g. "Put your budget where it converts".

11. **Negative parallelism in body copy** `sections.tsx:201` — copywriting: Negative Parallelism
    "We start with your data, not a template."
    Fix: "We start with your own account data."

12. **Repeated identical CTA** `sections.tsx:91, 487, 617` — R-15
    "Get a Proposal" three times. Not banned, but each one sits at a different point in the page
    and could name the actual next step (e.g. "Send us your ad account numbers").

13. **British/American spelling** — consistency only
    "optimises" and "optimisation" throughout. Deliberate is fine; just confirm it is deliberate.

## Not flagged (deliberately)

- "Learn more" inside the ad mockup `sections.tsx:275` — it is illustrating a generic ad, not a CTA of yours.
- "Scroll to see more" with an arrow `sections.tsx:374` — the arrow carries the direction, it is not decoration.
- Section headings using a bold span for one word — a consistent typographic motif, not slop.
